ABOUT ME

My name is Nithin Lalith. Born in Wisconsin but raised in Texas, I recieved me Bachelors of Science in Chemical Engineering from Texas Tech University in December 2024. While at TTU, I worked with Dr. Joseph Gauthier in the Chemical Engineering department to publish my first peer-reviewed paper as first co-author. I have also worked with Dr. Weilong Cong in the ISME department to reduce the defects in SiC silicon wafers caused by the micro-drilling process. I am looking forward to apply my engineering and research skills in a new, exciting environment.
